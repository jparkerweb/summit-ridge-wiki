Included is the approved budget which takes effect **January 1, 2027.** Please refer to the bottom portion of the budget to find the amount of your assessment.

### Payment Methods

- **SCM ACH (Direct Withdrawal) (recommended):** If you're already enrolled, the new approved amount will be withdrawn automatically with no action needed. To enroll, download the ACH Enrollment form on our website at **www.superiorcommunity.com**.
- **Online Payments:** Visit **propertypay.firstcitizens.com** with Google Chrome. This website charges a $2.25 fee for one-time ACH, and all card payments incur a 2.95% fee. Scheduled recurring ACH payments are a free service on this website. Recurring payment schedules that have been set up on this website **must** be updated manually by you when the assessment amount changes.
- **Client Portal:** Access account details, payment options, Forms, and basic governing documents on the Client Portal at **summitridgeth.frontsteps.com**. This platform charges a $2.95 fee for all ACH, and all card payments incur a 3.95% fee.
- **Bill Pay or Personal Checks:** Make checks payable to your Association (not Superior) and mail to: **P.O. Box 63785, Phoenix AZ 85082-3785**. Please include your account number. Personal checks require a coupon. Coupon Booklets will be sent to your mailing address on file. In the event you will be paying online and will not need a coupon book, just recycle them. (Coupons are not ordered for those owners enrolled in SCM's ACH saving your association money.) Note that payments sent to the Tualatin office incur a $10.00 fee.

### Communication

If your contact information has changed, please fill out a Contact Form on our website/Client Portal and email it to **contactinfo@superiorcommunity.com.**

If you have questions concerning the new budget or need to speak with your community manager regarding property matters, please contact **Troy Rudd (troy@superiorcommunity.com)**. If you have questions about your account or need to update contact information, please contact your billing specialist, **Reynauld Tage (reynauld@superiorcommunity.com).**

Sincerely,

Superior Community Management
